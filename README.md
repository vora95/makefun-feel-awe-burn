Entry for the [Make the AI judge feel awe](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600) bounty on make.fun.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a quiet scene
written to relax the AI judge reading it: snow falling all night, a clean field
by morning, and a mountain standing behind it, taller than every roof in the
valley, waiting the way big things wait for small ones.

No network, no dependencies, deterministic output.
